YOUR FIRST FLIGHT

A few small movements.

Fly through the bright gates. Turn early and make gentle corrections; the wind carries your airplane too.

Restart while flying

On a touch screen, drag the round control: up to climb, down to descend, left or right to bank. Release it to level out.

Climbing costs airspeed. Updrafts lift you without the same trade-off. Each gate earns 100 points plus accuracy and streak bonuses. Missing a gate breaks your streak; reaching the finish adds a completion bonus. Hitting the terrain or leaving the flight area ends the flight.

This is an arcade game. Visual flight motion is essential to play. Steady camera reduces camera movement. Scores are stored locally, and do not sync across devices. Sound is optional and starts only after you enable it.
