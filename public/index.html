YOUR FIRST FLIGHT

Find your line.

Fly through the bright gates. Turn early; gusts carry your airplane sideways. Bank around rock spires or climb above their summits.

On touch screens, drag the round control to fly and tap Roll when it is ready. A completed barrel roll earns 250 points and costs speed. Leave at least 35 m above terrain and room below the ceiling, and cover 200 m before the next roll. Holding Space does not repeat the trick.

Each gate earns 100 points plus accuracy and streak bonuses. Missing a gate breaks your streak; reaching the finish adds a bonus and unlocks the next difficulty. Replay saved levels for a better score, or generate fresh terrain. Free flight gets faster, windier and more crowded with distance; it does not advance course levels.

An arcade game, not aviation instruction. Steady camera reduces camera movement. The most recent 80 selected levels and 100 valid records stay in this browser when storage is available; they do not sync across devices. Sound is optional.
